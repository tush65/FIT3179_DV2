Internet Usage Across Australia

Digital technology has fundamentally shaped modern Australian life, showing how citizens engage in everyday activities such as banking, shopping, education, healthcare and more. This dashboard will explore how technology is actively being used in modern Australian society, while also showing some challenges, such as current gaps in usage across demographics.

To start, let us expand out and see how Australia compares to the rest of the world in terms of what percentage of its population uses the internet regularly. Clearly, we can see from this that Australia has one of the highest adoption rates in the world, even surpassing countries like the US, China, Russia, and most of Europe.

Within Australia

Now that we have shown the global trends, let is look inwards within the country.

New South Wales and Victoria clearly dominate with the sheer number of people accessing the internet, which is a result of thier high populations.

Meanwhile, the Australian Capital Territory leads the nation with usage rates of 93.6%, showing a much deeper green marker.

Why Do People Use The Internet

Now that we have seen how people use it in various states and territories, we will see the reasons for people accessing the internet. This radar chart shows a clear divide, with 4 out of 6 services having a high use rate of around 80%.

Services such as purchasing goods, social media, entertainment and banking show very high rates of use online. Digital platforms act as the primary way to watch entertainment and social media, streaming, and making connections.

On the other hand, health services and education show less digital integration, showing that medical consultations and formal learning often retain traditional in-person components.

What do Australians shop for?

Within shopping, we will now explore what people are buying in particular, split across many categories.

This follows the trends that were observed in the previous visualisation, with areas such as entertainment, cloting and travel showing relatively high use cases at around 50%.

However, areas such as Health, Cars, Food and Toys show quite low usage rates, all below 30%. This could be due to the fact that people prefer to see the physical items, such as Cars and Food before commiting to it, and people prefer to also see health professionals in person.

Technology Use Across Demographics

Let us now compare the different uses for technology across Age Group, to figure out who is the most tech savvy, and who is lagging behind.

Digital engagement peaks strongly among younder demographics. Particularly, those aged 18-34 dominate in areas such as banking and entertainment where usage exceeds 85%.

Conversely, traditional sectors like formal education show a sharp decline with age, as people being to do less formal education after their 20s, while health services show consistently low usage levels across all demographics, highlighting key areas for future digital growth.

Factors Influencing Access to Technology

Now that we have seen how internet usage shifts across age groups, let us delve deeper into this by seeing how internet usage differs across broader demographics.

We can see that overall, the internet usage rate even amongst the most disadvantaged demographic groups maintains a strong baseline of at least 65%, demonstrating idespread digital access across Australia.

Household income rates creates the biggest digital divide in Australia, with around 69% access rate for the lowest income bracket, and a near 100% usage rate for the highest earners. While this makes sense, some factors which are expected to play a big role, such as remoteness, show only a minor difference of around 7% in the usage rate.
